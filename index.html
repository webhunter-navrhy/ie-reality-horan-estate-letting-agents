Description

Horan Estates are proud to present this fantastic family home to the Dublin property market. This beautifully maintained three-bedroom semi-detached residence is ideally located within the highly sought-after Castleland development in North Dublin.

The property boasts bright and spacious accommodation throughout, presented in excellent condition and designed with comfortable family living in mind. Every detail has been carefully maintained, offering a home that is both stylish and practical. With its exceptional location close to local amenities, schools, transport links, and green spaces, this home truly enjoys a setting second to none.

The living spaces are thoughtfully designed. To the front of the property, the separate living room is bright and welcoming, enhanced by a large bay window that fills the space with natural light. At the rear, the modern kitchen-diner is perfect for family meals and entertaining, featuring sleek kitchen units and built-in appliances. Additional large storage room in the hallway add to the home’s practicality and charm. The main bedroom is truly luxurious, spanning the width of the property. It boasts a stylish ensuite and built-in wardrobes, offering ample storage space. The home is also energy-efficient, with an A2 energy rating and Air to water heat pump heating ensuring comfort and reduced energy costs. Occupying an impressive 125 square meters, its possible to further extend this property into the attic at a very reasonable cost also.

Castleland Park is a modern development that is perfectly positioned close to Balbriggan’s beautiful beach, charming harbour, and a wide range of town centre amenities, including schools, shops, and public transport links. This home truly has it all – style, comfort, energy efficiency, and a fantastic location. Whether you’re starting out or seeking to upgrade to a larger home, 11 Castleland Park Grove is the ideal choice - Approximately 10 min walk to the 101 bus stop (direct to Dublin Airport and city centre) - Approx 20 min walk to Balbriggan train station (commuter rail to Dublin city) - Approx 15 min by bus to Dublin Airport and close to 45 minutes to the city centre - Quick access to the M1 motorway

Viewing is a must and we would encourage those interested to contact as soon as possible as this beautiful property is unlikely to be available for long.
